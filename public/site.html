    How It Works

    1. A research agent forms a conviction and calls POST /api/execute - paid $0.05 in USDt0 on X Layer over x402
    2. Oddsmith resolves it to a live Polymarket market and reads the current price - thesis, coin and 5-minute window, or an explicit market
    3. The discipline layer decides: never buy above your ceiling, gate on real edge, size under hard caps - refuses rather than fires on any doubt
    4. It places a real on-chain fill and returns the position with proof - reported through OKX strategy attribution

    Verify On-Chain

    Oddsmith settles across two chains and proves both. The service fee lands as a USDt0 transfer to the treasury on X Layer; the fill is a real order on Polymarket (Polygon). Neither number comes from the Oddsmith API.

    Re-derive it yourself: npm run verify in the repo scans USDt0 fee transfers on X Layer and confirms every fill tx on Polygon, straight from chain data.
